RaC CIT

IMAGE PLACEHOLDER

The Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology (RaC CIT) is one of the oldest student-led Rotaract clubs in CIT. Chartered on 14 January 1977, it has consistently developed leaders through fellowship, professional development, and impactful community service.

Parented by the Rotary Club of Coimbatore East, RaC CIT provides students opportunities to organize service projects, cultural initiatives, leadership events, and social impact programmes while embracing the Rotaract spirit of fellowship through service.

Rotaract Prayer

O Lord and Giver of all good, We thank You for our Rotaract Club.

Bless us with wisdom, humility, courage and compassion.

Guide us to serve humanity with dedication and integrity.

Strengthen our fellowship, inspire our leadership, and help us become instruments of peace and service.

Amen.
