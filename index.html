About Us

Carrying the legacy of being one of the oldest clubs, Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology well known as RaC CIT was evoked in the year 1977 with the help of Rtn. P. R. Ramakrishnan, Founder of Coimbatore Institute of Technology and District Governor(1997) with the motto “Self development and Fellowship through Service”. The Club was chartered on 14th January 1977 with Charter President Rtr. PP. Shyam Sundar supported by its Parent Club, Rotary Club of Coimbatore East.

Rotaract Prayer

O God! Our Almighty Father and ruler of the Universe, We thank thee for the inspiration you have given us for the Rotaract Movement based upon Service to Mankind We humbly beg you to continue thy grace to enable us to do Our service, to ourselves and to our neighbours and to the honuor and glory of Thy holy name.

Logo Description

Harp – Represents unity and togetherness, with individuals coming together to create harmony, just as Rotaract brings people together with a common purpose.

4 Strings – Represent the four Avenues of Service. Each string holds its own note, but together they create a beautiful melody, symbolising how every avenue comes together to create the harmony of Rotaract.

Wings – Symbolise growth, freedom and limitless possibilities available for young Rotaractors.

51 – Represents 51 years of the club, marking a new chapter beyond the Golden Jubilee while carrying forward its legacy.

Rotary Wheel – Embedded within the 51, it represents our Rotary roots, values and enduring spirit of service.

The curved line above the club name represents the legacy and foundation of the club.

The curved line below the club name represents the journey forward and future it holds.
